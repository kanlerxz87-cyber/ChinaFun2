A practical seven-night draft — not a qualified transit itinerary

  1. Mon Feb 15: arrival, check-in, light Bund / Nanjing Road walk. Avoid Monday-closed museums and Yu Garden.
  2. Tue Feb 16: Shanghai Museum People’s Square after checking the day/evening session and any reservation notice; individual walk-in rules have exceptions. Flexible neighborhood dinner.
  3. Wed Feb 17: Yu Garden daytime and old-town walk; do not confuse bazaar/night-tour tickets with garden admission.
  4. Thu Feb 18: neighborhood culture and one prebooked tasting-menu dinner, if budget and dietary requirements fit.
  5. Fri Feb 19: Shanghai Tower, subject to visibility, ticket selection and route confirmation.
  6. Sat Feb 20: flexible food day; event closures and crowds require a fresh venue-calendar check.
  7. Sun Feb 21: optional exhibition or neighborhood walk; leave packing and transfer time.
  8. Mon Feb 22: checkout/departure. No paid intercity side trip assumed.

All hotel-to-site and hotel-to-restaurant travel remains unverified. See official activity schedules and restaurant sources before assigning reservations.
