Executive summary

Shanghai base · 7 nights · 2 adults · private bathroom + queen/king bed · all dining & activities within ~1 hour by metro/bus/rail.

📅 Recommended dates

Mon 15 Feb → Mon 22 Feb 2027

7 nights, Monday-to-Monday. Starts 2 days after Spring Festival ends (13 Feb) + makeup workday (14 Feb), so you dodge the 9-day holiday crowds and peak pricing while still catching the Lantern Festival tail (20 Feb) in Suzhou/Yu Garden if you want it.

Alternatives: Tue 16 Feb, Wed 17 Feb, Thu 18 Feb starts (all 7 nights) — see Costs & Booking for the full 6-option table. Early-March (1 Mar, 15 Mar) is warmer/drier if you prefer spring weather over late-winter atmosphere.

🏨 Recommended hotel area

Bund / People's Square (Huangpu)

Highest Michelin density within 1 hr (est. 25–28 starred restaurants), walkable to the Bund, Yu Garden and Nanjing Road, and Lines 1/2/8/10/14 put every activity within ~30 min. Top-ranked: Hyatt on the Bund, The Peninsula Shanghai, Marriott Marquis City Centre — see Hotels for the full ranking. Pudong (Grand Hyatt Jin Mao) is best for skyline views but weakest for dining density.

⭐ Dining strategy

1 splurge + 2 value stars

Splurge: Taian Table (3★, ¥2,888/person, couple ¥5,776 ≈ $865) — book 90 days ahead, ¥2,000/person deposit. Value stars: Canton 8 (2★, ~¥202–350/person) and Lao Zheng Xing (1★, ~¥148/person) — world-class dining for a couple at ¥296–700 total. Fill gaps with Bib Gourmand (164 listed) and Selected (168 listed) picks. Full table: Michelin.

12 activities, all ≤ 1 hr

Free core (Bund, Nanjing Road, Xintiandi, French Concession, Shanghai Museum) + ticketed highlights (Oriental Pearl Tower ¥199, Shanghai Tower ¥180, Yu Garden ¥40) + 2 rail day trips (Suzhou ~25 min, Hangzhou ~45–60 min). Museums/gardens close Mondays — plan accordingly. Full table: Activities.

What to do, and when

  • Now (Nov–Dec 2026): Apply for US L-visa ($140, 10-year multi-entry) — no flights/hotels needed. Shortlist hotels; set price alerts.
  • ~90 days out (mid-Nov 2026 for 15 Feb): Book Taian Table (opens 90 days ahead; ¥2,000/person deposit). Book refundable hotel placeholder when inventory opens (~3 months out).
  • 2–3 months out (Dec 2026–Jan 2027): Lock free-cancellation hotel rate (Booking.com sweet spot).
  • 8–14 days out (1–7 Feb 2027): Re-check hotel price (Hotels.com sweet spot, ~23% saving vs 4+ months out). Rebook if cheaper, cancel original inside free-cancellation window.
  • Before travel: Book Shanghai Museum timed ticket (free, WeChat), Yu Garden / tower tickets, Suzhou/Hangzhou G-trains.
