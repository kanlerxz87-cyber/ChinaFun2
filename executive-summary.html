A closure-aware seven-night Shanghai draft — not a route-verified itinerary

  1. Mon Feb 15: arrival, check-in, Xintiandi / Nanjing Road walk and Bund illumination (17:00–21:00 winter schedule). Monday-closed venues (Yu Garden, Power Station of Art, People’s Square Museum, Taian Table, Da Vittorio) are avoided.
  2. Tue Feb 16: Yu Garden classical daytime garden (¥60 for two, 09:00–16:30) and Power Station of Art (¥0 general entry, 11:00–19:00). Note Shanghai Museum East is closed Tuesdays.
  3. Wed Feb 17: Shanghai Museum East Campus in Pudong (10:00–18:00, walk-in passport entry ¥0) paired with Shanghai Tower 118F observatory (¥360 for two, visibility permitting).
  4. Thu Feb 18: Wukang / Anfu Road walk + prebooked Michelin meal with published couple menu pricing (e.g., T’ang Court ¥888 dim sum lunch / ¥2,288 set, Yi Long Court ¥695 lunch / ¥2,304 dinner, or Taian Table ¥5,176–¥5,776 tasting).
  5. Fri Feb 19: Shanghai Disneyland full day (from ¥950 for two on official resort tier calendar) or neighborhood Bib Gourmand crawl (Da Hu Chun, Mi Thai, Yuge · Zhanjiang).
  6. Sat Feb 20 (Lantern Festival 2027): check crowd/event surcharges before visiting Old City / Yuyuan; consider Bund dining (Bao Li Xuan ¥3,576 degustation or Sir Elly’s ¥3,004 5-course set).
  7. Sun Feb 21: flexible French Concession / Jing’an day or optional Zhujiajiao excursion (Kezhi Garden ¥40 for two; >60m suburban trip).
  8. Mon Feb 22: checkout/departure (or high-speed rail onward to Beijing/Chengdu/Guangzhou if extending to 14–28 nights with an eligible visa).
