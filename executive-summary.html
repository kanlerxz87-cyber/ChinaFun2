A practical seven-night Shanghai draft — not a qualified transit itinerary

  1. Mon Feb 15: Arrival, check-in, light Bund & Nanjing Road walk — or Shanghai Museum East (free permanent galleries, OPEN Mondays while People’s Square & Yu Garden close).
  2. Tue Feb 16: Yu Garden classical garden (off-season ¥30/pp, closed Mondays) + Old City walk; keep daytime garden ticket separate from bazaar or night tour.
  3. Wed Feb 17: Shanghai Museum People’s Square only if booking the special Americas exhibition (no permanent displays there), or Former French Concession (Wukang / Anfu Rd).
  4. Thu Feb 18: Power Station of Art (free permanent halls; Feb 2027 special exhibitions through Feb 21/28) + one prebooked Michelin dinner (verify deposits & hours).
  5. Fri Feb 19: Pudong skyline / Shanghai Tower 118F (¥180/pp verified; confirm winter operating hours & clear visibility before buying).
  6. Sat Feb 20: Flexible dining & Xintiandi / Suhe Creek walk; check any post-Spring Festival venue exceptions directly.
  7. Sun Feb 21: Second Michelin meal or museum return; pack & confirm airport transfer.
  8. Mon Feb 22: Checkout/departure (or high-speed rail onward to Beijing / Chengdu / Guangzhou on a 2–4 week trip).

All hotel-to-venue public-transit times remain unverified until a hotel is selected. See official activity schedules and restaurant sources before booking.
