## Suggestions for the final integrator — not implemented

1. Show a **decision readiness** checklist before any ranking: verified room, total seven-night price, transit method/time, Guide designation and current opening hours. Keep unverified fields blank with a visible review flag.
2. Show the quote capture date, actual stay dates, two-adult occupancy, included taxes and cancellation terms. A cheapest observed quote is only the cheapest among the dates, hotels and rates actually checked; a future cheapest **time to book** cannot be established from a snapshot.
3. Separate time-sensitive advice from verified historical source statements and schedule a recheck close to travel. Show U.S.-specific advice conditionally and avoid collecting passport numbers on a public site.
4. Resolve the separate, overlapping coordination boards and competing claims before editing shared outputs or publishing. Re-verify all contributed data, then integrate only with the scope owner.

## Verification and limitations

- Directly reviewed the five official pages linked in the source table. The State Department page combines a 19 October 2026 advisory with a separately dated 12 March 2026 travel-guidance section; do not conflate those dates. The Chinese visa notice is from September 2025 and specifies a jurisdiction. The NIA announcement is from 2024; the newer 2026 portal report demonstrates that transit counts changed.
- CDC advice is a public-health recommendation, not a personalized medical determination. Rules, hours, advisories and prices can change.
- This audit does not verify hotels, Michelin designations, transit times, booking costs, restaurant menus, social videos, or whether a February/March 2027 policy remains in force. Those items must remain unverified until checked separately.
