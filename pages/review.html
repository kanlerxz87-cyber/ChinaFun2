FLAGGED FOR REVIEW

Irregularities & limitations

  • Hotel totals and availability: official booking pages did not expose a verifiable 2027 seven-night quote in the research response. No lowest-price claim is possible.
  • Travel times and Michelin counts: no official, date-specific route matrix was obtained. A shared district is not a 60-minute route.
  • Restaurant total, hours and social links: missing fields remain unverified. Published guide categories cannot be converted into a bill for two.
  • Room bathrooms: hotels describe bathrooms, but privacy for a specific bookable room has not been separately confirmed.
  • Changing information: 2026 Michelin distinctions and current schedules may not apply in 2027. Holiday exceptions must be checked. Independent videos are labelled and do not verify venue facts.
  • Scope: Shanghai is a starting point, not proof it is the best city in China. The shortlist and activity list are not exhaustive.
