METHODOLOGY

What counts as a real ranking?

First inventory the full current Michelin selection, including starred, Bib Gourmand and listed restaurants. For each hotel, verify transit routes for the same representative dates and times, including walking and transfers, then count distinct venues with door-to-door journeys of 60 minutes or less. Keep stars and non-star listings separate.
