WHAT A TOTAL MUST INCLUDE

Like-for-like, two adults

Use one room, 2 adults, the same bed class, all 7 nights, taxes/fees included, and the same cancellation terms. A one-night “from” rate or an unpriced room category is not a verified total.

08 / AUDIT TRAIL

Sources & review flags

Source links are placed beside each data item. Official source means the relevant hotel, Michelin, museum, government or venue page—not a third-party rate aggregator.

Line-by-line source discipline

Research principles

  • Blank means “not confirmed from the linked official source,” not zero.
  • Hotel cost is not estimated from generic “from” pricing, ratings, or unrelated dates.
  • Transit durations and Michelin catchment counts are not inferred from straight-line distance.
  • Michelin selection year is shown explicitly; Bib Gourmand and Selected are distinct from Stars.
  • Direct YouTube reviews, official Instagram profiles and TikTok videos are labelled distinctly from search fallbacks.

Flagged for review

  • Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou or Chengdu: no destination preference was supplied.
  • Official date-specific total for 7 nights: unavailable in static snapshots across brand booking engines.
  • Five of 16 hotels (WAF-gated Hilton/Renaissance room pages + Sofitel/St. Regis modals) still need checkout bath confirmation.
  • One-hour transit catchments and Michelin restaurant counts per hotel: not computed without live metro route logs.
  • Shanghai Museum People’s Square permanent galleries are closed for renovation Jul 2026–Nov 2027 (use Pudong East Campus; closed Tuesdays). Chen Clan Ancestral Hall in Guangzhou is also closed Tuesdays.
  • Hong Yu Fang’s official MICHELIN page gives conflicting schedule details (daily 10:00–22:00 vs “morning only”), and Fabula differs on Tuesday hours between website and Guide.
