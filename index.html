08 / AUDIT TRAIL

Sources & review flags

Every record links directly to its official hotel, Michelin, museum or municipal source—plus clearly labelled third-party social reviews where verified.

Line-by-line source discipline

Research principles

  • Blank (—) means “not confirmed from the linked official source,” never zero.
  • Hotel cost is never estimated from undated “from” widgets, pre-tax screenshots or third-party aggregators.
  • Transit durations and ≤60-minute Michelin catchments are never guessed from straight-line distance.
  • Michelin selection year and distinction (Star vs Guide Selected) are always shown explicitly.
  • Direct YouTube, TikTok and Instagram links are labelled as third-party reviews (or official venue/museum accounts) and never override official facts.

Key flags open for review

  • Base city and trip length (1–2 weeks vs 2–4 weeks) remain open; stays >10 days require visa/visa-free verification (entry audit).
  • Official 2027 all-in hotel totals and literal private-bathroom guarantees require live booking-engine capture.
  • Shanghai Museum People’s Square hosts only the ticketed Americas exhibit through Nov 2027; use Shanghai Museum East for free permanent galleries.
  • Shanghai Tower & Oriental Pearl operating hours are blanked pending manual verification on official pages.
  • T’ang Court (Langham) has a Monday hours contradiction; Hong Yu Fang has a morning-only contradiction; Ritz-Carlton Pudong (Jin Xuan) has Oct 2026 renovation notices.
