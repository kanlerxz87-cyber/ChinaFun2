08 / AUDIT TRAIL

Sources & review flags

Source links are placed beside each data item. Official source means the relevant hotel, Michelin, museum, government or venue page—not a third-party rate aggregator.

Line-by-line source discipline

Research principles

  • Blank means “not confirmed from the linked official source,” not zero.
  • Hotel cost is not estimated from “from” pricing, ratings, or unrelated dates.
  • Transit durations and Michelin catchment counts are not inferred from straight-line distance.
  • Michelin selection year is shown explicitly; a guide listing is not automatically a star.
  • Direct creator videos are linked only where public page metadata and venue/content were checked; videos are not official evidence for current hours, prices or Michelin status. Search/profile links are discovery aids only.

Flagged for review

  • Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou or Chengdu: no destination preference was supplied.
  • Official date-specific total for 7 nights: unavailable in this snapshot.
  • Private-bathroom status is not explicit on the reviewed official room pages.
  • One-hour transit catchments and Michelin restaurant counts per hotel: not computed.
  • Activity and restaurant exceptions for February 2027: not announced in cited sources.
  • Hong Yu Fang’s official MICHELIN page gives conflicting schedule details: daily 10:00–22:00 in the hours table, “morning only” in the description.
