Executive Summary

This dashboard organizes verified travel data for a 1–2 week couple's trip to China between February 15, 2027 and March 23, 2027 (with a preferred window starting February 15–18, 2027). Because the trip prioritizes proximity (≤ 1 hour by subway or bus) to Michelin-starred and Michelin Guide restaurants as well as major cultural activities, we evaluated Mainland China's two densest Michelin clusters: Shanghai and Beijing.

  • Best Primary Hub (1-Week Trip): Shanghai (Huangpu / The Bund) — highest Michelin density in Mainland China (1 Three-Star, 12 Two-Star, 38 One-Star in the 2026 Guide), with central hotels within 15–45 minutes by Metro/bus of major clusters and top sights (Yu Garden, Shanghai Museum, The Bund).
  • Best Dual-Hub Option (2-Week Trip): Shanghai (7 nights) + Beijing (7 nights) — Beijing's 2027 Michelin Guide (announced Oct 28, 2026) features 2 Three-Star, 5 Two-Star, and 26 One-Star restaurants, with Wangfujing/Dongcheng hotels within 10–30 minutes of the Forbidden City and Chaoyang dining clusters.
  • Pricing & Booking Window Status: Official hotel websites use dynamic booking engines that do not expose static 2027 rates to crawlers. Exact 7-night totals for Feb 15–22, 2027 and cheapest upcoming booking windows are left blank and flagged on Flagged for Review for live booking-engine lookup.
  • Important Date Note: February 15–18, 2027 falls immediately after Chinese New Year (Feb 6, 2027), meaning some attractions and restaurants may have holiday hour exceptions—confirming exact 2027 schedules is flagged.
