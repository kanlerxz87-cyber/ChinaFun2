08 / AUDIT TRAIL

Sources & review flags

Source links are placed beside each data item. Official source means the relevant hotel, Michelin, museum, government or venue page—not a third-party rate aggregator.

Line-by-line source discipline

Research principles

  • Blank means “not confirmed from the linked official source,” not zero.
  • Hotel cost is not estimated from “from” pricing, ratings, or unrelated dates.
  • Transit durations and Michelin catchment counts are not inferred from straight-line distance.
  • Michelin selection year is shown explicitly; a guide listing is not automatically a star.
  • Video links are YouTube search shortcuts, not verified/endorsed creator uploads.

Flagged for review

  • Shanghai vs Beijing selection: no destination preference was supplied.
  • Official date-specific total for 7 nights: unavailable in this snapshot.
  • Private-bathroom status is not explicit on the reviewed official room pages.
  • One-hour transit catchments and Michelin restaurant counts per hotel: not computed.
  • Activity and restaurant exceptions for February 2027: not announced in cited sources.
  • Hong Yu Fang’s official MICHELIN page gives conflicting schedule details: daily 10:00–22:00 in the hours table, “morning only” in the description.
  • Earlier detailed subpages remain in the repository, but several use proxy pricing, secondary sources or estimated transit counts; they are not treated as verified by this landing dashboard. Read the audit flags ↗
  • Social-media creator links: no individual restaurant/hotel posts could be verified as official; the museum’s official profiles are linked.
How to interpret city Michelin cluster counts

The Beijing MICHELIN Guide 2026 release is a city selection and reports 32 starred restaurants (2 three-star, 6 two-star, 24 one-star). Shanghai’s 2025 city selection reports 52 starred (2 three-star, 9 two-star, 41 one-star). The later 2026 Shanghai–Jiangsu–Zhejiang regional release counts 77 stars across a larger combined geography. The 2025 Shanghai-city and 2026 regional totals are not a same-year, same-area comparison; this page does not use them to declare a winner.
