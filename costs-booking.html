5 · Practical essentials

🧧 Spring Festival 2027

5–13 Feb 2027 (9 days), Lunar New Year Sat 6 Feb. Makeup workdays Sun 31 Jan + Sun 14 Feb. Your 15 Feb start is after the holiday — good. Holiday calendar ↗

🛂 Visa (US passport)

Need L tourist visa for round-trip tourism: $140, 10-year multi-entry, 4 business days (2–3 express). No flights/hotels/itinerary needed since Jan 2024. Alternative: 240-hour visa-free transit only with onward ticket to a third country/region. Guide ↗

🌧️ Weather (Shanghai)

Feb: high ~10.7°C / low ~4.1°C, ~65mm, 10.4 wet days — cold + damp; warm coat, layers, waterproof shoes. Mar: high ~14.9°C / low ~7.6°C, ~97mm, 12.7 wet days — changeable; light jacket + umbrella. Packing guide ↗
