A perfect 7-day rhythm (15–22 Feb)

  • Mon 15 — Arrive. Bund at dusk. Canton 8 dinner (2★, best value).
  • Tue 16 — Shanghai Museum (09:00). Lao Zheng Xing lunch. Xintiandi evening.
  • Wed 17 — Yu Garden (09:00 sharp) + bazaar. Fu He Hui dinner (2★ vegetarian).
  • Thu 18 — French Concession walk. Ji Pin Court lunch. T'ang Court dinner*.
  • Fri 19 — Pudong: Shanghai Tower + Ocean Aquarium. Da Vittorio dinner.
  • Sat 20 — Lantern Festival: Yu Garden bazaar lights (expect crowds). Taian Table (3★) — book first.
  • Sun 21 — Natural History Museum or Bao Li Xuan lunch. Bund farewell walk.
  • Mon 22 — Depart. *Check Monday closures: Shanghai Museum, Yu Garden, Natural History Museum, Da Vittorio, Vivant are shut Mondays; T'ang Court Monday status needs confirmation.

Good to know

  • Metro: Lines 2 & 10 link the Bund ↔ People's Square ↔ Jing'an ↔ Lujiazui. All activities above are ≤30 min from the Bund except Changning/Xuhui dining (~35 min max).
  • Booking: Shanghai Museum + Natural History Museum need timed WeChat reservations (overseas visitors can use Trip.com). Yu Garden, Shanghai Tower and the Aquarium sell timed tickets online.
  • Weather: Pack a down jacket, layers, hat/gloves/scarf and waterproof shoes. Heated interiors + cold damp streets = dress in removable layers.
