Executive summary

The one-page decision brief. Everything below is detailed (with source links) on the Hotels, Restaurants, Activities and Dates pages. Research date 25 Nov 2026.

The trip in one paragraph

Go to Shanghai, stay 7 nights in Huangpu, Mon 15 – Mon 22 Feb 2027. Shanghai beats Beijing for this brief because it has materially more Michelin dining within one subway ride (2026: 51 starred of 156 listings, including the sole 3-star, Taian Table) and the densest cluster sits within a few metro stops of the Bund. Fly in, base once, and every researched restaurant and activity is ≤60 minutes by subway/bus — most are ≤25 minutes. The preferred 15 Feb start lands just after the Chinese New Year holiday (5–13 Feb) and includes Lantern Festival (Sat 20 Feb) at Yu Garden.

Hotel decision

  • Best value: Hyatt on the Bund — king room, ~$805 / 7 nights indicative (¥5,369), 18/20 researched Michelin within 1h.
  • Best rated near Yu Garden: Crystal Hotel (Yu Garden) — queen room, ~$1,260 / 7 nights indicative, 4.9/5 sample rating.
  • Splurge: Waldorf Astoria on the Bund (~$2,590 indicative) or Shanghai EDITION (~$2,261) — same Michelin reach, Bund views.
  • Only Pudong option: Park Hyatt (~$1,246 indicative, 17/20 within 1h) — pick for skyline, not for Puxi dining speed.

All six Huangpu hotels tie on Michelin reach (18/20) — so the real tie-breakers are live 7-night total, cancellation terms and metro exit. Full ranking → Indicative ≠ locked: confirm the live total for 15–22 Feb before paying.

Restaurant decision

  • Book first (sells out): Taian Table — 3★, ¥5,176–5,776 for two (verified). Tue–Sat dinners only.
  • Best value 2★: Canton 8 — ~¥404–800 for two. Best value 1★: Lao Zheng Xing ~¥296–370 for two.
  • Most distinctive: Fu He Hui (2★ vegetarian, ¥1,776–1,998 for two); Fabula (1★, tiny attic — book 4–6 weeks out).
  • Never book: Ultraviolet — closed 29 Mar 2025. Sir Elly's & Mr & Mrs Bund are Selected, not starred — fine to visit, don't expect stars.
