Do not book Ultraviolet. It closed permanently on 29 March 2025 (final service) and was removed from the guide. It is listed at the bottom only to prevent booking errors — any site selling Ultraviolet reservations is a scam risk. Shanghai's sole 3-star is now Taian Table.

Clusters at a glance

  • Huangpu (Bund / Xintiandi / People's Square) — densest: 102 House, Otto e Mezzo, Canton 8, Da Vittorio, Imperial Treasure, T'ang Court, Vivant, Lao Zheng Xing + Sir Elly's & Mr & Mrs Bund (Selected). Stay here = most dinners ≤20 min by metro.
  • Jing'an: Bao Li Xuan, House of Rong, Tou Zao, Fabula, Huaiyang Delights. ~10–15 min from the Bund by Line 2/13.
  • Changning / Xuhui: Taian Table (3★), Fu He Hui, Amazing Chinese Cuisine, Ji Pin Court. ~20–35 min from the Bund — still well within 1 hour.
