Methodology — how the “within 1 hour” counts work

  • Sample: The 20 restaurants on the Restaurants page (all Shanghai 3★/2★ in the 2026 guide + two notable 1★ + two Selected + the closed Ultraviolet). The full guide has 156 Shanghai restaurants; counting all 156 per hotel is listed as a future improvement on the Suggestions page.
  • Rule: A restaurant counts if the hotel → restaurant journey is ≤60 min by metro/bus including a walk of ≤10 min at each end, using Lines 2/10/13/14 as the main corridors. Huangpu hotels reach 18/20 (the Changning villa outposts are the marginal >60-min cases at peak). Park Hyatt (Lujiazui) is 17/20 because Puxi west is one metro ride longer.
  • Not counted as travel: taxis (traffic-variable) and walking the whole way. Your 1-hour constraint is subway/bus — this method follows it exactly.
  • Research-truth rule: Star status, official hours and official ticket prices are marked Verified. Anything from secondary listings alone stays Needs review and is listed on the Flagged page.

Festival & weather context (verified dates)

  • Chinese New Year: Sat 6 Feb 2027. Public holiday Fri 5 – Sat 13 Feb 2027. Your preferred start (15 Feb) is after the holiday — good for crowds, but confirm restaurant holiday rosters.
  • Lantern Festival: Sat 20 Feb 2027 — Yu Garden's lantern show is the seasonal highlight and the busiest night of the trip. Book that day's restaurant early and expect the bazaar to be packed.
  • February weather (secondary): ~2–9 °C, damp cold, occasional drizzle. Down jacket + layers + waterproof shoes. Viewpoint (Shanghai Tower) is best on the first clear morning after rain.
